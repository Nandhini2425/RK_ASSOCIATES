RK ASSOCIATES WEBSITE

Files
- index.html: Website content and sections
- styles.css: Responsive visual design
- script.js: Mobile navigation, poster gallery filters, WhatsApp callback form
- assets/rk-logo.jpg: RK Associates logo supplied by the company
- assets/*poster.jpg: Posters shown only in the separate Gallery module

How to open
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. Keep the assets folder beside index.html; do not move the images separately.

Sections
Home, About Us, Our Services, Documents, Providers, Gallery and Contact Us.
Service shortcuts lead to their document checklists. Aadhaar and PAN are marked mandatory. The callback form opens a prefilled WhatsApp message for the visitor to review and send.
