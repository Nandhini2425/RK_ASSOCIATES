BEFORE YOU APPLY

Documents checklist

Document requirements vary by product, lender, insurer and customer profile. Aadhaar and PAN are mandatory for initial verification with RK Associates; the provider may request additional documents.

✓
Mandatory for initial verification

Aadhaar Card and PAN Card

Loans · Required documents

Personal, business/MSME, gold, home, vehicle and loan against property

  • Aadhaar Card — Mandatory
  • PAN Card — Mandatory
  • Recent salary slips (commonly 3 months)
  • Bank statement (commonly 3–6 months)
  • Employment / office ID proof, if requested
  • Mobile number and email ID
  • Address proof, if requested

Business / MSME Loan

  • Aadhaar Card — Mandatory
  • PAN Card — Mandatory
  • Business proof / registration / licence
  • Bank statements (last 6–12 months)
  • ITR / financial statements, if requested
  • Business address and ownership proof
  • Co-applicant / guarantor documents, if applicable

Loan Against Property

  • Aadhaar Card — Mandatory
  • PAN Card — Mandatory
  • Property deed / title documents
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC), if requested
  • Patta / tax receipts / approved plan, as applicable
  • Bank statements and income proof
  • Legal and valuation documents requested by lender

Gold / Home / Vehicle Loans

  • Aadhaar Card — Mandatory
  • PAN Card — Mandatory
  • Address and income proof, if required
  • Gold for valuation / property documents / vehicle quotation as applicable
  • Bank statements, if requested
  • Co-applicant documents, if applicable

Insurance · Required documents

  • Aadhaar Card — Mandatory
  • PAN Card — Mandatory
  • Mobile number and email ID
  • Nominee details
  • Health / lifestyle information for life or health cover, as requested
  • Vehicle RC and existing policy for motor insurance

Chits · Required documents

  • Aadhaar Card — Mandatory
  • PAN Card — Mandatory
  • Bank account details / cancelled cheque, if required
  • Photograph, mobile number and email ID
  • KYC and nomination details as required by provider
  • Additional forms based on product rules

Investments · Required documents

  • Aadhaar Card — Mandatory
  • PAN Card — Mandatory
  • Bank account details / cancelled cheque, if required
  • Mobile number, email ID and photograph
  • KYC, nominee and risk-profile details, where applicable
  • Additional forms required by the specific provider

Please share documents only through an authorised and secure channel after confirming the provider and purpose. Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords. Final requirements are set by the respective provider.
